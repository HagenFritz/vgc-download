### Type Vulnerability Audit

**Ice** — hits Garchomp (4x Dragon/Ground), Aerodactyl (2x Rock/Flying), Meganium-Mega (2x Grass/Fairy). This is the team's only stacked weakness and it's severe. Mega Froslass's spread Blizzard — the format's current identified "tech to beat" — hits all three simultaneously for 4x/2x/2x in one turn. Aerodactyl's Wide Guard buys one turn. After that, no answer. Incineroar (Fire resists Ice) is the only soft check. No Ice immunity, no additional Ice resist.

No other type clears the 3+ member threshold. Ground and Rock each hit 2 members; Dark and Fairy each hit 2 members.

### Structural Gaps

**Over-covered:**
- Two spread Rock Slide users (Garchomp + Aerodactyl) with overlapping weaknesses — both go down to the same Ice or Ground coverage. When the opponent runs Wide Guard (Pelipper 68.7%, Araquanid 77.7%), both primary spread tools are blocked simultaneously.
- Two speed control tools (Aerodactyl Tailwind + Milotic Icy Wind) with no TR component — fully committed to the speed game. One Gale Wings Tailwind or one Armor Tail TR setup flips both tools off simultaneously.
- Four protective moves across 6 slots (Milotic, Garchomp, Meganium-Mega Protect + Aegislash King's Shield) — leans heavy on stall turns, relatively passive pressure profile.

**Under-covered:**
- **TR disruption — none.** No Taunt, no Imprison, no Mold Breaker. Farigiraf (93.3% TR, 11.48% usage) is the #3 most-used Pokemon in the format and sets TR against this team 100% of the time. This is a structural loss to ~10% of the field.
- **Fighting coverage — none.** Zero Fighting moves across 6 Pokemon. Kingambit gets free Defiant boosts from Incineroar's entire support kit. Garchomp EQ is the closest answer (Ground hits Kingambit 2x) but requires positioning.
- **Aurora Veil answer — none.** No Brick Break, no Defog. Ninetales-Alola successfully setting Veil means 8 turns of halved physical damage against a physically-leaning team with no counterplay.
- **Weather — none.** Meganium-Mega's Weather Ball and Solar Beam are dead moves without Sun. No Drought, no Sunny Day on the entire team.
- **Items on 3 of 6 Pokemon — missing.** Milotic, Aegislash, and Incineroar have no items. This is the most immediate construction gap — all three are significantly underperforming without items.

### Overall Verdict

The team has a genuine identity: Aerodactyl Tailwind offense with Milotic Competitive as the Intimidate punisher and Meganium-Mega as a surprise Grass/Fairy Mega attacker alongside the solid Garchomp + Incineroar spine. It handles Rain and Balance matchups adequately and has the speed control to compete with opposing Tailwind teams. The three critical deficiencies that independently decide games against common archetypes are: (1) zero TR disruption against the format's second most common setup structure — a structural loss to at least 10% of the field, (2) three missing items making Milotic, Aegislash, and Incineroar significantly underoptimized, and (3) Meganium-Mega running two dead moves (Weather Ball and Solar Beam) without any Sun source, wasting 143 base SpA. With TR disruption added, items completed, and Meganium-Mega's moveset corrected, this core has real tournament potential. As submitted for the Global Challenge, it loses to every dedicated TR team it faces.
