# TR Team Draft — Pokemon Champions Regulation M-A — 2026-04-27-002 (Revised)

## Strategy Summary

Hard-leaning Semi-Room built around Mega Golurk as the primary TR abuser and Competitive Milotic as the dedicated Incineroar punish. The revision swaps **Hatterene → Sinistcha** (Rage Powder solves Sneasler-pre-TR, Matcha Gotcha solves Rain, Hospitality keeps Mega Golurk healthy, Grass/Ghost breaks the Dark stack) and **Tinkaton → Aerodactyl** (Rock STAB + Tough Claws answers Charizard-Y / Sun, Tailwind provides a real Plan B). Two TR setters (Sinistcha + Farigiraf Armor Tail) preserve iron-clad Taunt and Sucker-Punch answers. Item Clause now clean — Sitrus only on Farigiraf.

## User Constraints

- **Preference string:** "Mega Golurk as primary TR abuser with Headlong Rush + Competitive Milotic to counter Incineroar"
- **User-specified picks:** Golurk-Mega — Primary TR Abuser (Headlong Rush) / Milotic — Competitive anti-Incineroar pivot
- **Architect choices:** Sinistcha (primary TR setter, Hospitality, Rage Powder), Farigiraf (backup TR setter, Armor Tail), Aerodactyl (Rock STAB / Tailwind Plan B), Weavile (fast Plan B, Fake Out, Ice priority)

## Win Conditions

1. **Primary:** Sinistcha sets TR turn 1 (Rage Powder redirects Sneasler CC / Incineroar Knock Off mid-setup) → Mega Golurk in turn 2 → Headlong Rush + Poltergeist deletes the Incineroar / Kingambit / Sneasler / TTar / Archaludon skeleton. Hospitality from Sinistcha offsets Headlong Rush Def drop and chips Mega Golurk back to full.
2. **Secondary:** Aerodactyl Tailwind Plan B with Rock STAB. When TR is denied (Imprison, Whimsicott Encore, Sinistcha KO'd before TR), Aerodactyl Tailwind + Rock Slide flinch pressure carries the game. Weavile Fake Out + Triple Axel + Ice Shard works alongside.
3. **Anti-Meta:** Competitive Milotic punishes the meta's #1 ability (Incineroar Intimidate, 51% usage) for free +2 SpA. Sinistcha Rage Powder + Hospitality make Mega Golurk effectively immortal under TR. Aerodactyl Rock Slide is the dedicated Charizard-Y / Volcarona / Sun-archetype answer.

## Roster Breakdown

### Sinistcha
- **Role:** Primary TR setter / redirection / Grass STAB / Hospitality support
- **Why this pick:** Highest-leverage swap on the team. **Rage Powder redirects Sneasler CC / Incineroar Knock Off** — directly solves the pre-TR-bypass hole. **Matcha Gotcha** is the Rain-archetype answer (Grass STAB into Pelipper, Basculegion 2x). **Hospitality** passes 25% HP + 1 Def stage to Mega Golurk — keeps the abuser healthy through Headlong Rush self-Def-drop and chip damage. Grass/Ghost typing **breaks the triple-Dark stack** (now only Farigiraf + Mega Golurk weak to Knock Off, instead of three).
- **Loss vs. Hatterene:** No Magic Bounce. Mitigated by Mental Herb (one-shot Taunt protection) + Farigiraf Armor Tail backup.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 196 Def / 60 SpD Sassy 0 Spe IV. Physically bulky to survive Sneasler CC if redirection is read around. Sassy 0 Spe underspeeds Farigiraf cleanly when both are out for TR ordering.

### Golurk-Mega [USER PINNED]
- **Role:** Primary TR abuser / spread Ground wallbreaker
- **Why this pick:** User-pinned and well-suited. Iron Fist pre-Mega boosts Ice Punch by 20% (KOs Garchomp/Dragonite). On Mega, ability becomes **Unseen Fist** — contact moves bypass Protect, which is enormous since every Incineroar/Kingambit Protects on the TR-set turn. Ground/Ghost typing also: immune to Fake Out (Normal), immune to Sucker Punch (Ghost). Headlong Rush spreads Ground STAB on the entire opposing field except Flying/Levitate.
- **Key interactions:** Don't lead Golurk — bring it in turn 2 after Sinistcha sets TR. Sinistcha Hospitality offsets Headlong Rush Def drop. Farigiraf Helping Hand + Headlong Rush guarantees the OHKO on Mega Tyranitar / bulky Incineroar. Poltergeist hits Sinistcha mirrors (4x Ghost) and item-holding Floette-Mega.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD Brave 0 Spe IV. Underspeeds the entire format under TR.

### Milotic [USER PINNED]
- **Role:** Competitive Incineroar punish / Icy Wind speed control / bulky Water pivot
- **Why this pick:** User-pinned, cleanest anti-meta pick in the format. Incineroar is on 51% of teams; Competitive snags +2 SpA every Intimidate switch-in, turning Scald into a meta-deleting button. Icy Wind is the underused-yet-correct speed control (Garchomp / Dragonite / Charizard-Y all 4x Ice). Recover keeps her alive across multiple Intimidate cycles.
- **Key interactions:** Do NOT bring Milotic vs no-Incineroar matchups (Rain HO, Sun without Incineroar, hard TR mirror). Double-target Incineroar pivots with Mega Golurk Headlong Rush + Milotic Scald. Icy Wind globally cripples Tailwind speed mode.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 196 Def / 60 SpD Calm 0 Atk IV. Bulky pivot. **Acknowledged tradeoff:** 101 effective Speed is slower than max Rotom-W (106) and Floette-E (112) for Icy Wind — accepted for survival vs Sneasler CC and TTar Stone Edge.

### Farigiraf
- **Role:** Backup TR setter / Armor Tail anti-priority / Helping Hand support
- **Why this pick:** Armor Tail blocks ALL priority — Kingambit Sucker Punch (99% of sets), Aqua Jet, ESpeed, and Prankster Taunt from Whimsicott. Backup TR if Sinistcha is doubled. Imprison is a tech option for Hard TR mirror.
- **Key interactions:** Pair with Mega Golurk for the Sucker-Punch read — Farigiraf Armor Tail shields the Mega Golurk swap-in turn. Helping Hand + Headlong Rush is a guaranteed Mega TTar OHKO.
- **EV rationale (revised for clean Sitrus):** **244 HP / 132 Def / 132 SpD Sassy 0 Spe IV**. HP lands at 226 → halves cleanly to 113 → Sitrus triggers at exactly the half-HP threshold. Mixed bulk for two-sided priority absorption.

### Aerodactyl
- **Role:** Tailwind Plan B / Rock STAB anti-Charizard / fast cleanup
- **Why this pick:** Solves the Charizard-Y / Sun matchup hole. Base 130 Speed outruns the entire unboosted meta. **Rock Slide** with Tough Claws + Hard Stone OHKOs Charizard-Y (4x), 2HKOs Whimsicott / Aerodactyl mirrors / Volcarona. **Tailwind** is the off-TR speed mode that makes Mega Golurk usable when TR is denied. Wing Attack handles Sun's Venusaur partner and Whimsicott (2x each).
- **Why base (not Mega):** Mega slot is locked to Golurk. Base Aerodactyl with Tough Claws is still the fastest legal Rock attacker in the format.
- **EV rationale:** 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe Jolly. 178 effective Speed — outruns everything unboosted. Hard Stone gives ~20% Rock damage without Life Orb recoil (Life Orb not in champions_items.json).

### Weavile
- **Role:** Plan B fast attacker / Fake Out / Ice priority / Knock Off
- **Why this pick:** Base 125 Speed Fake Out lead under Plan B. Triple Axel one-shots Garchomp / Dragonite / Hippowdon. Knock Off cripples item-dependent picks (Sinistcha Mental Herb, Sneasler White Herb, Whimsicott Sash). Ice Shard cleans Multiscale Dragonite survivors and revenge-kills weakened Mega Aerodactyl.
- **Key interactions:** Lead Weavile + Aerodactyl vs Tailwind Offense — Fake Out Aerodactyl, Tailwind from our Aero, then Triple Axel cleanup. Knock Off into Sneasler before White Herb fires.
- **EV rationale:** Standard 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe Jolly. Focus Sash for guaranteed Ice Shard on the cleanup turn.

## Bring-4 Guidelines

### vs. Sun (Charizard-Y + Venusaur + Incineroar + Whimsicott)
**Bring:** Aerodactyl + Sinistcha + Mega Golurk + Milotic
- Lead **Aerodactyl + Sinistcha**. Aerodactyl Rock Slide turn 1 — Tough Claws + Hard Stone, 4x on Charizard-Y = OHKO range. Sinistcha sets TR or Rage Powders Sleep Powder.
- Turn 2: Mega Golurk in for Headlong Rush on Incineroar / TTar back row.
- Milotic Icy Wind cleanup (4x on Charizard if alive, drops Whimsicott Tailwind tier).
- **Bench Weavile** (Heat Wave 2x) and **Farigiraf** (Solar Beam pressure under Drought).

### vs. Rain (Pelipper + Archaludon + Basculegion + Incineroar)
**Bring:** Sinistcha + Aerodactyl + Farigiraf + Weavile
- **Bench Mega Golurk and Milotic** — both have 4x electric/water exploits. Structural cost of pinned core.
- Lead Sinistcha + Aerodactyl. Matcha Gotcha into Pelipper 2x; Rock Slide flinches Pelipper out of rain.
- Turn 2: Farigiraf TR backup if needed. Weavile Knock Off Archaludon, Ice Shard finishes Bascu.
- 40-50 matchup — playable, was an autoloss in v1.

### vs. Trick Room Mirror (Farigiraf + Sinistcha + Torkoal/Kingambit)
**Bring:** Aerodactyl + Weavile + Mega Golurk + Milotic
- **Don't set TR.** Lead Aerodactyl + Weavile. Aerodactyl Tailwind, Weavile Fake Out the opposing Sinistcha (Armor Tail blocks Fake Out on opposing Farigiraf, so target Sinistcha).
- Turn 2: Weavile Knock Off Sinistcha (removes Mental Herb, opens Poltergeist). Aerodactyl Rock Slide spread.
- Mega Golurk in late under their TR (we're slow under their TR — favors us as the abuser). Milotic Icy Wind for post-TR cleanup.

### vs. Tailwind Offense (Whimsicott + Aerodactyl + Sneasler + Garchomp)
**Bring:** Sinistcha + Mega Golurk + Aerodactyl + Milotic
- Lead **Sinistcha + Mega Golurk**. **Sinistcha Rage Powder turn 1 redirects Sneasler CC** away from Mega Golurk. Mega Golurk Ice Punch into Garchomp (4x) under TR.
- Aerodactyl Tailwind backup if Sinistcha goes down before TR sets.
- Milotic Icy Wind drops opposing Tailwind tier on cleanup.

## Threats and Weaknesses

### Hard counters (structural to user pins)
- **Rain HO with Archaludon Electro Shot.** Milotic 4x Electric, Mega Golurk 4x Water. Both pinned. **Mitigation:** bench both, lean Sinistcha + Aerodactyl + Farigiraf + Weavile (40-50 matchup, no longer autoloss after Sinistcha swap).
- **Sucker Punch Kingambit on the setup turn.** Farigiraf Armor Tail solves *only when leading*. If Sinistcha leads and Kingambit Sucker Punches into TR turn, that's a clean read against us. **Mitigation:** lead Farigiraf into visible Kingambit; Sinistcha as Plan-B redirection.

### Soft checks (uncomfortable but playable)
- **Imprison Farigiraf** locks our TR plan since both Sinistcha and Farigiraf rely on TR. Plan B (Aerodactyl Tailwind) bypasses but loses the Mega Golurk wincon.
- **Mega Floette CM snowball.** Aerodactyl Rock Slide neutral, no Knock Off on Sinistcha. Weavile Knock Off + Ice Shard chip is the answer.
- **Triple-Dark spam (Knock Off Incineroar / Sneasler / TTar)** still erodes Farigiraf and Mega Golurk. Reduced from 3 → 2 weak members vs v1.
- **Milotic Speed (101) is slower than max Rotom-W (106) and Floette-E (112)** for Icy Wind. Acknowledged tradeoff; bulk over speed for Sneasler/TTar survival.
- **Mega Excadrill Sand offense** still has no clean Ground immunity on the team. Aerodactyl Rock Slide handles Excadrill (neutral, but Tough Claws). Mitigation: Milotic Scald + Aerodactyl flinch math.

### Pinned-pick weaknesses (USER PREFERENCE — known tradeoffs)
- **Mega Golurk takes the Mega slot** from a more flexible piece (Mega Hatterene faster TR setter, Mega Tyranitar weather control). Tradeoff accepted for Headlong Rush + Poltergeist + Unseen Fist into the meta's exact weak axis. **Mega Golurk is 4x Water/Grass/Ghost/Dark/Ice** — Sinistcha Matcha Gotcha (mirror), Basculegion Wave Crash, Weavile Knock Off all 2HKO. Pre-Mega 80/80 defenses leave it vulnerable on the setup turn — never lead Golurk.
- **Competitive Milotic sits dead vs no-Incineroar matchups** (Rain HO, Sun-without-Incineroar, hard TR variants). Bring rate ~60% of matchups. Milotic 4x Grass (Sinistcha mirror) and 4x Electric (Archaludon). Bench her vs Rain HO unless Sneasler is the Intimidate bait.

### Tech options
- **Sinistcha Mental Herb → Rocky Helmet** if Knock Off pressure becomes overwhelming.
- **Farigiraf Hyper Voice → Imprison** for the Hard TR mirror lockout.
- **Aerodactyl Wing Attack → Iron Head** for Floette / Whimsicott pressure.
- **Weavile Ice Shard → Throat Chop** for Perish Trap (Mega Gengar) silencing.
