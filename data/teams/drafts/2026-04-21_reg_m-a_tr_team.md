# TR Team Draft — Regulation M-A — 2026-04-21

## Strategy Summary

This is a hard Trick Room team built around the Hatterene + Farigiraf dual-setter core. Hatterene's Magic Bounce reflects Taunt back at the opponent; Farigiraf's Armor Tail blocks all priority moves including Fake Out on the setup turn. The primary game plan is to establish TR on turn 1 behind Farigiraf's Armor Tail immunity and Hatterene's Taunt reflection, then abuse Ground spread (Rhyperior Earthquake) and Fighting priority (Conkeldurr Mach Punch) to sweep. The Plan B mode deploys Mega Gardevoir as a fast special attacker outside TR — Timid Gardevoir outspeeds nearly everything in the format and provides Rain coverage (Energy Ball), Fairy spread (Dazzling Gleam), and anti-Sneasler pressure (Psychic). This team specifically targets the format's dominant Big Three (Incineroar / Sneasler / Garchomp) through Psychic 4x coverage, Ground spread, and Fairy spread pressure.

## Win Conditions

1. **Primary:** Set TR with Farigiraf (Armor Tail) or Hatterene (Magic Bounce) on turn 1. Bring in Rhyperior (Earthquake spread) and Conkeldurr (Drain Punch + Mach Punch). Sweep the Ground-weak field — Incineroar, Sneasler, Kingambit, Tyranitar, and Archaludon are all hit hard by EQ. Farigiraf Helping Hand amplifies Rhyperior or Conkeldurr KO thresholds.

2. **Secondary (Plan B — Fast Mode):** Lead Mega Gardevoir + Incineroar. Gardevoir outspeeds and threatens the entire format at Timid nature. Incineroar Fake Out buys Gardevoir a free turn. Dazzling Gleam spreads Fairy pressure; Energy Ball covers Pelipper/Basculegion/Rotom-Wash; Psychic erases Sneasler. This mode is specifically viable against Taunt-heavy leads that would otherwise shut down both setters.

3. **Anti-Meta:** Farigiraf Imprison tech specifically locks opposing Sinistcha from using TR in the mirror. Hatterene Magic Bounce shuts down Whimsicott's Prankster Tailwind (Taunt reflected back) and Rotom-Wash Will-O-Wisp. Rhyperior Ground spread punishes the format's single-sourced Levitate distribution (only Rotom-Wash runs Levitate in the top 20). Gardevoir Psychic 4x-hits Sneasler — the format's number 2 threat.

## Roster Breakdown

### Hatterene
- **Role:** Primary TR setter, offensive Psychic/Fairy threat, anti-Taunt wall
- **Why this pick:** Magic Bounce is the strongest anti-Taunt ability in the format. Whimsicott (17% usage) and any Prankster Taunt user gets the move reflected back at them. Hatterene's base 136 SpA under TR makes her a legitimate wincon — Psychic 4x-hits Sneasler (48% usage), Dazzling Gleam spread threatens Garchomp (38%), Dragonite (11%), and Tyranitar. Specifically targets the format's most dominant physical threat. Protect is added to survive turn 1 focus-fire when not leading — Sneasler Fake Out + Kingambit Sucker Punch would otherwise KO Hatterene before she can act.
- **Key interactions:** Pairs with Farigiraf for the dual-setter core. When Farigiraf leads and takes Fake Out (Armor Tail blocks it), Hatterene can use TR on the same turn uncontested. Incineroar Fake Out in back gives a free TR turn as well. Life Orb elevates Hatterene's damage into genuine OHKO range on Sneasler and near-OHKO on Garchomp.
- **EV rationale:** 244 HP survives common attacks and minimizes Life Orb recoil. 196 SpA reaches solid damage benchmarks — Psychic OHKOs Sneasler, Dazzling Gleam 2HKOs Garchomp. 60 Def patches physical frailty slightly. 0 Atk IV removes confusion damage; 0 Spe IV ensures she moves last outside TR, acting first inside TR.
- **Changes from previous draft:** 0 Atk IV added (Finding #7). Protect replaces the slot that was previously occupied by a 4th attacking move — Hatterene had no protection against focus-fire in the original draft (implicit fix from the dual-setter review).

### Farigiraf
- **Role:** Secondary TR setter, Imprison tech, Helping Hand support
- **Why this pick:** Armor Tail is the single best TR-enabling ability in the format. Fake Out from Incineroar (51% usage) and Sneasler (89% Fake Out rate) is completely nullified — Farigiraf sets TR on turn 1 against the dominant archetypes. Mental Herb cures the first Taunt. Imprison tech in the 4th slot is the mirror-TR answer — locking Sinistcha (31% usage, 68% TR rate) and opposing Farigiraf from flipping speed. Armor Tail also blocks Sucker Punch from Kingambit (28% usage), which would otherwise threaten a KO on the setter.
- **Key interactions:** The dual-setter pair with Hatterene means the team can lose one setter and still establish TR. Helping Hand + Rhyperior Earthquake or Conkeldurr Drain Punch creates guaranteed KO thresholds. Hyper Voice provides spread damage that Intimidate cannot reduce.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP maximizes bulk on the setter. 116 Def + 100 SpD creates mixed bulk to survive spread attacks — survives Garchomp Rock Slide and Sneasler Dire Claw. 36 SpA provides marginal Hyper Voice damage. Sassy nature and 0 Spe IV for TR priority.
- **Changes from previous draft:** 0 Atk IV added (Finding #7).

### Rhyperior
- **Role:** Primary physical TR abuser, Ground/Rock spread damage
- **Why this pick:** The format's Ground-weak concentration is massive — Incineroar, Sneasler, Kingambit, Tyranitar, Archaludon are all Ground-neutral or Ground-weak. Spread Earthquake from base 140 Attack under TR is the team's primary damage output. Solid Rock reduces super-effective hits by 25%, making Rhyperior surprisingly durable against Grass and Water coverage. Rock Slide threatens Charizard-Y (4x weak to Rock) and adds flinch pressure. Rock Wrecker is the single-target nuke (150 BP, effectively 300 with STAB) for guaranteed KOs. High Horsepower is the critical alternate Ground option for Wide Guard scenarios.
- **Key interactions:** High Horsepower specifically addresses the Pelipper Wide Guard problem. Pelipper runs Wide Guard on 34% of sets — EQ and Rock Slide are both spread moves that Wide Guard blocks simultaneously. High Horsepower is a single-target move that bypasses Wide Guard entirely, allowing Rhyperior to threaten Archaludon or Basculegion through Pelipper's Wide Guard. Farigiraf Helping Hand + High Horsepower secures KOs in the Rain matchup. Assault Vest provides special bulk to tank Archaludon Electro Shot chip.
- **EV rationale:** 220 HP + 252 Atk + 36 Def. 0 Spe IV for TR priority.
- **Changes from previous draft:** Item corrected from "Assault Vault" to "Assault Vest" (Finding #8).

### Incineroar
- **Role:** Fake Out support, Intimidate chip, pivot
- **Why this pick:** Fake Out + Intimidate controls turn 1 — flinch the most dangerous target, drop both opponents' Attack. Parting Shot maintains momentum while dealing chip. In this TR context, Incineroar enables Hatterene or Farigiraf to set TR on turn 1 without disruption. Throat Chop blocks voice moves (opposing Farigiraf Hyper Voice in mirrors; disables Basculegion switch-in with Wave Crash).
- **Key interactions:** Intimidate on switch-in from Parting Shot creates chaining pressure. Flare Blitz answers Mega Froslass and threatens Mega Scizor. Sitrus Berry activates at 50% HP for the pivot gameplay — switch in, take a hit, Sitrus fires, pivot out.
- **Speed note (Finding #4):** Kingambit minimum speed under TR is 49 (base 50, 0 IVs, 0 EVs, hindering nature). Incineroar minimum is 58. Under TR, Kingambit moves BEFORE Incineroar, meaning Sucker Punch or Kowtow Cleave lands before Fake Out. Managed by: (a) avoid leading Incineroar when Kingambit shows in team preview, (b) use Conkeldurr Mach Punch (priority +1, bypasses TR order entirely) as the Kingambit punish instead, (c) Farigiraf Armor Tail on the field blocks Sucker Punch entirely.
- **EV rationale:** 244 HP / 68 Atk / 100 Def / 96 SpD totals 508. HP reaches 152 (divisible by 4) — Sitrus Berry heals exactly 38 HP for clean activation. Defensive split survives Garchomp Rock Slide and Sneasler spread. Brave nature and 0 Spe IV.
- **Changes from previous draft:** EV spread corrected to 244 / 68 / 100 / 96 for clean Sitrus Berry activation at HP 152 (Finding #6).

### Gardevoir (Mega)
- **Role:** Fast-mode Plan B sweeper, Rain coverage, anti-Sneasler nuke
- **Why this pick:** Mega Gardevoir is the team's only fast Pokemon and the Plan B when TR is denied. Timid Gardevoir outspeeds everything in the format's top 20 except Aerodactyl (base 130) and unboosted Sneasler. Psychic 4x-hits Sneasler. Dazzling Gleam spreads Fairy damage. Energy Ball is the Rain coverage — hits Pelipper, Basculegion, and Rotom-Wash for super-effective Grass damage, which addresses the format's 12-15% Rain archetype that the original draft completely ignored. Protect is mandatory — without it, Sneasler Fake Out + Kingambit Sucker Punch deletes Gardevoir before it acts, making the fast-mode Plan B non-functional.
- **Key interactions:** Gardevoir + Incineroar is the fast-mode lead. Fake Out the most dangerous target; Gardevoir Protects; turn 2 Gardevoir fires freely. In Rain matchups, Energy Ball Pelipper on turn 1 removes Wide Guard + Drizzle. Trace copies Intimidate from opposing Incineroar (free +2 Attack for a special attacker is irrelevant, but Intimidate drops become Trace activations), Rough Skin for free chip, or Unburden from post-White Herb Sneasler.
- **EV rationale:** 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP. Timid nature maximizes speed tier. 0 Atk IV removes confusion damage. No defensive investment — Gardevoir's role is to threaten KOs before taking hits; one Protect buys the turn.
- **Changes from previous draft:** Energy Ball replaces Hyper Voice (Finding #1 — CRITICAL Rain coverage). Protect added as 4th move (Finding #2 — CRITICAL mandatory Protect on doubles sweeper). 0 Atk IV specified (Finding #7).

### Conkeldurr
- **Role:** Secondary physical TR abuser, priority cleanup, Guts sweep
- **Why this pick:** Guts + Flame Orb gives a 1.5x Attack multiplier once the burn activates turn 2. Under TR, Conkeldurr at base 45 Speed moves first. Close Combat is the strongest Fighting STAB for bursting targets. Drain Punch provides recovery for longevity. Mach Punch (+1 priority) is critical for out-of-TR play and specifically answers Kingambit — priority Fighting bypasses TR speed order entirely, winning the Sucker Punch exchange from Conkeldurr's side. Ice Punch covers the 4x Ice weakness of Garchomp and Dragonite, the two most common spread physical threats.
- **Key interactions:** Mach Punch directly answers Kingambit outside TR without depending on Incineroar Fake Out. Drain Punch sustains through chip damage. Pairs with Farigiraf Helping Hand for boosted Drain Punch KOs on bulkier targets. Flame Orb activates on the bench if Conkeldurr is switched in, giving the Guts boost starting from the next turn.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP maximizes longevity. 252 Atk maxes damage output. 4 Def. Brave nature and 0 Spe IV for TR priority.

## Bring-4 Guidelines

**vs. Rain HO (Pelipper + Basculegion + Archaludon):**
Bring Farigiraf + Rhyperior + Gardevoir + Incineroar. Do not lead both setters against Rain — Gardevoir is the dedicated Rain answer. Lead Gardevoir + Incineroar fast-mode: Incineroar Fake Out Basculegion (removes Aqua Jet priority and chip from Scarf Last Respects); Gardevoir Energy Ball Pelipper turn 1. Pelipper dies to Energy Ball — Drizzle ends, Wide Guard is gone. Turn 2: bring in Farigiraf if the board is favorable for TR setup (Archaludon and remaining Rain mons are slower than typical TR abusers). Rhyperior High Horsepower targets Archaludon (bypasses any remaining Wide Guard from a second Pelipper partner if applicable). High Horsepower + Farigiraf Helping Hand is the Archaludon KO. The key principle: remove Pelipper first, then TR becomes viable as Rain's speed advantage collapses.

**vs. Tailwind Offense (Whimsicott + Aerodactyl + Sneasler + Garchomp):**
Bring Hatterene + Farigiraf + Rhyperior + Incineroar. Lead Hatterene + Farigiraf. Hatterene Magic Bounce reflects Whimsicott Taunt; Farigiraf Armor Tail blocks Sneasler Fake Out on Hatterene. Farigiraf uses Imprison turn 1 (blocks Sinistcha if present) or sets TR; Hatterene attacks or sets TR as backup. Under TR, Garchomp (Scarf outspeeds everything under normal speed, becomes slowest under TR) and Sneasler (effective 240 speed, first under normal, last under TR) are completely reversed. Rhyperior EQ threatens both. Keep Incineroar in back — Parting Shot recovery, Fake Out on Aerodactyl leads if needed.

**vs. Sun (Charizard-Y + Venusaur + Whimsicott):**
Bring Farigiraf + Incineroar + Rhyperior + Hatterene. Lead Farigiraf + Incineroar. Incineroar Fake Out Charizard turn 1; Farigiraf sets TR. Under TR, Rhyperior Rock Slide 4x-hits Charizard. Hatterene Psychic is neutral on Venusaur (Grass/Poison — Psychic is not SE on Grass but is SE on Poison, so Psychic hits Venusaur SE). Incineroar Parting Shot reduces Venusaur's Sludge Bomb damage. Critical: TR setup is mandatory in this matchup — Sun teams outspeed the entire team without TR. Do not play Fast Mode against Sun.

**vs. Semi-Room Balance (Incineroar + Sinistcha + Sneasler core):**
Bring Farigiraf + Conkeldurr + Incineroar + Hatterene. Lead Farigiraf + Incineroar. Farigiraf uses Imprison to lock Sinistcha from using TR — this is the defining play of the matchup. Incineroar Fake Out the most dangerous front-row target. Under TR, Conkeldurr Fighting STAB 4x-hits Incineroar. Hatterene Psychic 4x-hits Sneasler. With Imprison preventing TR reversal, the field stays in your TR as long as Farigiraf lives. If Sinistcha tries Rage Powder, accept the redirect and use the turn to switch in a better attacker.

## Threats and Weaknesses

**Hard counters:**
- **Mega Floette-E at +2 Calm Mind with Fairy Aura.** This is the team's most uncomfortable matchup. Dazzling Gleam at +2 under Fairy Aura (1.33x damage modifier) threatens every Pokemon on this roster. Incineroar is Dark-type, meaning it is Fairy-weak — Moonblast OHKOs it after a CM. Neither setter resists Fairy. The team's plan: prevent the setup. Use Incineroar Parting Shot on the CM turn to reduce Floette-E's SpA; Gardevoir Psychic (neutral on Fairy but 165 SpA at Timid speed) before it reaches +2; Conkeldurr Mach Punch for priority chip. This is a manageable matchup before +2, an autoloss after +2 without a switch-in that resists Fairy. Tech option: replace Ice Punch on Conkeldurr with Poison Jab (Iron Fist-boosted to effective ~105 BP SE on Fairy = strong answer) if Floette-E becomes dominant locally. This costs Garchomp/Dragonite 4x Ice coverage.
- **Opposing Imprison + TR on a dedicated anti-mirror setter.** If the opponent leads with Imprison + TR on Oranguru or a second Farigiraf, both setters can be locked simultaneously. Hatterene and Farigiraf must each absorb the Imprison check independently. Plan B (Gardevoir fast mode) is the fallback — do not panic and attempt TR against a confirmed Imprison user.

**Soft checks:**
- **Kingambit Sucker Punch.** Sucker Punch is +1 priority Dark — it bypasses TR speed order and hits both Hatterene (2x, Psychic type) and Farigiraf (2x, Dark-weak). However, Farigiraf's Armor Tail blocks all priority moves, so Sucker Punch on Farigiraf specifically is nullified. Hatterene must Protect if Kingambit is the active threat. Gardevoir Dazzling Gleam puts neutral pressure on Kingambit from the Fast Mode. Conkeldurr Mach Punch is priority Fighting vs Kingambit — 4x weak to Fighting.
- **Charizard-Y Heat Wave under Drought.** Threatens both Hatterene and Farigiraf. Rhyperior Rock Slide under TR is the primary answer (4x on Zard). The critical window is turn 1 before TR is established. Lead Farigiraf + Incineroar vs Charizard — Fake Out Charizard to buy the TR setup turn.
- **Mega Froslass Aurora Veil + Blizzard.** Aurora Veil halves damage on the opponent's side. Veil teams slow the sweep significantly. Incineroar Flare Blitz OHKOs Froslass. Hatterene Magic Bounce reflects Icy Wind or status moves if they try to support the sweep.

**Tech options:**
- **Poison Jab on Conkeldurr** (over Ice Punch): Converts Conkeldurr into the team's dedicated Floette-E answer. Iron Fist-boosted Poison Jab (effective ~78 BP * 1.3 Iron Fist = ~101 BP, SE on Fairy = ~202 effective) gives reliable damage. Recommended if Floette-E variants are dominant in the local meta. Costs Garchomp/Dragonite 4x Ice coverage.
- **Colbur Berry on Farigiraf** (over Mental Herb): Halves Dark-type damage, specifically Kingambit Sucker Punch. Trades Taunt immunity for Sucker Punch survival on the primary setter. Run this if Kingambit is over-represented locally.
- **Protect on Farigiraf** (over Imprison): Adds defensive utility at the cost of mirror-TR tech. Run Imprison in metas with heavy Sinistcha usage; run Protect in more open field metas where the mirror is less likely.

## Critique & Revisions

### Meta Coverage Checker Summary
The initial draft had a critical gap against Rain HO (Pelipper + Basculegion + Archaludon, 12-15% of the meta). No Grass or Electric coverage existed anywhere on the six-Pokemon team. Pelipper Wide Guard simultaneously blocks both of Rhyperior's spread moves (EQ and Rock Slide). Archaludon Electro Shot under rain is an instant-charge spread nuke threatening both setters. Basculegion Scarf Last Respects snowballs endgames through chip-damaged back rows. The team additionally lacked coverage for Rotom-Wash, which walls Rhyperior and threatens both setters with Hydro Pump. Mega Floette-E's Calm Mind endgame was flagged as an IMPORTANT weakness given 19% usage and zero Fairy-resistant Steel-types on the team.

### TR Viability Checker Summary
Composition failures before revision: (1) No Protect on Mega Gardevoir — the fast-mode Plan B was non-functional, as Sneasler Fake Out + Kingambit Sucker Punch would remove Gardevoir before it acts. (2) No coverage for the Rain archetype. Composition passes: dual TR setters with distinct denial counterplay (Magic Bounce + Armor Tail/Mental Herb), Imprison mirror-TR answer, Ground spread abuse with Wide Guard bypass (High Horsepower), Fake Out answer via Armor Tail, spread moves across multiple slots, item diversity (Life Orb / Mental Herb / Assault Vest / Sitrus Berry / Gardevoirite / Flame Orb — six distinct items, no duplicates).

### Speed Math Auditor Summary
EV totals verified legal across all six Pokemon. The critical catch: Incineroar's previous spread (244 HP / 60 Atk / 100 Def / 100 SpD = 504 total) produced HP stat 151 — not divisible by 4, meaning Sitrus Berry heals 37.75 HP (rounds to 37) rather than the clean 38. Corrected to 244 HP / 68 Atk / 100 Def / 96 SpD (total 508), producing HP 152 for clean Sitrus activation. Pure special attackers (Hatterene, Farigiraf, Gardevoir) were all missing 0 Atk IV specifications, which is standard for any Pokemon that never uses physical moves.

### CRITICAL Issues That Triggered Revision
1. **No Rain coverage (Finding #1):** Energy Ball replaces Hyper Voice on Gardevoir. This single move addresses Pelipper (removes Wide Guard / Drizzle with an SE hit), Basculegion (Grass 2x on Water), and Rotom-Wash (Grass 2x on Water). Rain was a 12-15% metagame archetype with zero answer in the original draft.
2. **No Protect on Gardevoir (Finding #2):** Protect added as 4th move. Since Energy Ball replaced Hyper Voice (the 3rd move slot), Protect became the natural 4th slot without losing any other coverage. Both CRITICAL findings were resolved simultaneously with no net move-count reduction.

### IMPORTANT / POLISH Findings Documented
- **Finding #3 (Floette-E):** Documented as the team's primary hard counter. Poison Jab tech option listed. No Pokemon swap performed per explicit constraint — "do not sacrifice Rain coverage or Protect to fix this."
- **Finding #4 (Incineroar vs Kingambit speed under TR):** Documented in Incineroar's roster entry. Managed through Conkeldurr Mach Punch (priority +1 bypasses TR order) and team preview bring-4 decisions. Not a structural failure — a known format quirk.
- **Finding #5 (Charizard-Y):** Documented in Soft Checks. Sun bring-4 guidelines address the setup priority sequencing.
- **Finding #6 (Incineroar EV spread):** Corrected to 244 HP / 68 Atk / 100 Def / 96 SpD for HP 152, enabling clean Sitrus Berry activation.
- **Finding #7 (0 Atk IV):** Added to Hatterene, Farigiraf, and Gardevoir.
- **Finding #8 (item typo):** Assault Vest corrected on Rhyperior.
