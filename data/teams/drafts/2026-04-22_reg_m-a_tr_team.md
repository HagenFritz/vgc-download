## Strategy Summary

Dual-setter Trick Room exploiting the Ground-weak and Psychic/Fairy-weak skeleton dominating early Reg M-A. Hatterene is the primary setter — Magic Bounce reflects the format's most common TR denial (Prankster Taunt). Farigiraf backs up with Armor Tail (blocks Fake Out from Incineroar/Sneasler on the setup turn) and Imprison (locks Sinistcha's TR in the most common archetype). Rhyperior and Conkeldurr are the physical TR kill-switches. Mega Gardevoir is the Plan B fast attacker that also carries Energy Ball — the team's answer to Rain HO.

No Covert Cloak in Champions means Sinistcha Rage Powder has no item counter. The team answers this by KO'ing Sinistcha proactively (Hatterene Psychic, Conkeldurr Close Combat) and locking her TR with Farigiraf Imprison, rather than trying to ignore redirection.

## Roster Breakdown

### Hatterene @ Twisted Spoon | Magic Bounce | Quiet | 0 Atk / 0 Spe

**Role:** Primary TR setter / offensive attacker.

**Why this pick:** Magic Bounce reflects Prankster Taunt — the format's #1 TR denial — back onto the Taunt user. Hatterene cannot be Taunted by Prankster, making her effectively guarantee TR unless the opponent has a non-Prankster Taunt user. At base 136 SpA with Twisted Spoon boosting Psychic by 20%, she is a real offensive threat: Psychic OHKOs Sneasler (4x weakness, Twisted Spoon amplifies), Dazzling Gleam threatens Garchomp, Dragonite, Tyranitar, and Kingambit. Base 29 Speed makes her the slowest relevant attacker in the format — she moves first under TR and last everywhere else.

**Key interactions:** Lead with Farigiraf to activate Armor Tail on Hatterene's turn. Hatterene sets TR while Farigiraf's Armor Tail blocks incoming Fake Out. Protect is the safe-entry move when position is unclear.

**EV rationale:** 244 HP gives meaningful bulk. 196 SpA gives strong offensive output — Twisted Spoon picks up the damage slack Life Orb would have provided, without recoil. 60 Def pads the physical defense. 4 EVs in SpD. 0 Atk / 0 Spe IVs: standard for a Quiet TR setter.

**Item note:** Twisted Spoon is the legal Champions item that most closely replicates the role Life Orb would have played — Psychic damage boost (20%) on Hatterene's key move for the 4x Sneasler kill. No recoil, which means Hatterene survives longer TR cycles.

### Farigiraf @ Mental Herb | Armor Tail | Sassy | 0 Atk / 0 Spe

**Role:** Secondary TR setter / support / Imprison mirror tech.

**Why this pick:** Armor Tail blocks ALL priority moves, including Fake Out. When Farigiraf is on the field, Incineroar and Sneasler Fake Out cannot flinch any Pokemon on Farigiraf's side. This is the cleanest TR setup insurance available — it removes the format's most common disruption in one ability slot. Mental Herb cures the first Taunt if one sneaks through a non-Prankster source. Imprison + Trick Room in hand locks Sinistcha (68% TR rate) and opposing Farigiraf from reversing TR mid-game.

**Key interactions:** Lead alongside Incineroar when you want Fake Out control from your side (Incineroar Fake Out flinches their Sneasler, Farigiraf sets TR). Lead alongside Hatterene when you want the full anti-denial suite (Magic Bounce + Armor Tail).

**EV rationale:** 252 HP maxes bulk. 116 Def / 100 SpD spread survives Sneasler Close Combat and Garchomp EQ on the setup turn. 36 SpA gives minor Hyper Voice chip — could be moved to Def if damage output is not needed. 0 Atk / 0 Spe IVs standard for a Sassy TR setter.

---

### Rhyperior @ Passho Berry | Solid Rock | Brave | 0 Spe

**Role:** Primary physical TR abuser / Ground + Rock spread attacker.

**Why this pick:** The format's Ground-weak stack is the biggest structural exploit in the meta: Incineroar, Sneasler, Kingambit, Tyranitar, and Archaludon all eat Earthquake. At base 140 Atk with Brave + full investment, Rhyperior is hitting at 211 Attack. Solid Rock reduces super-effective damage by 25%. Passho Berry halves the first incoming Water-type attack — this is the critical tech against Rain HO, where Basculegion Wave Crash under rain and Archaludon's rain-boosted pressure otherwise OHKO Rhyperior at 4x Water weakness. Solid Rock (0.75x) × Passho (0.5x) = 0.375x effective Water damage on the first hit — turning an OHKO into a healthy tank. High Horsepower bypasses Pelipper Wide Guard (Wide Guard blocks spread moves — EQ and Rock Slide — but not single-target moves).

**Key interactions:** Pair with Farigiraf Helping Hand for guaranteed KO thresholds. Do NOT pair with physical attackers who also take Earthquake damage — positioning awareness is required (Rhyperior EQ hits adjacent allies). Base 40 Speed = calculated 40 Spe, moves first under TR against all meta threats above Kingambit (49 min Spe).

**Item note:** Passho Berry is the direct legal answer to the 4x Water weakness flagged by the viability-checker. Assault Vest is not legal in Pokemon Champions — the Champions item list is restricted to 117 items and does not include competitive SpD-boost items.

**EV rationale:** 252 Atk maximizes output. 220 HP gives meaningful survivability. 36 Def pads physical defense. EV total exactly 508.

### Incineroar @ Sitrus Berry | Intimidate | Brave | 0 Spe

**Role:** Fake Out support / Intimidate chip / momentum pivot.

**Why this pick:** The format's glue — on 51% of teams for a reason. Fake Out flinches the most dangerous opposing lead, protecting the TR setter's setup turn. Parting Shot maintains Intimidate cycling and pivots fresh abusers in safely. Throat Chop hard-counters Sinistcha's Matcha Gotcha (sound move) while also preventing opposing Hyper Voice support. Dark-type means Prankster moves (Whimsicott Taunt/Encore) cannot target it.

**Key interactions:** Lead alongside either setter. Fake Out the opposing Fake Out user (Sneasler, opposing Incineroar) to clear the turn for TR setup. Parting Shot as the Kingambit punish is the format's most efficient Defiant-counter — catch Parting Shot with Kingambit → Kingambit gets +2 Atk from Defiant → Parting Shot into a new Pokemon cancels the punish.

**EV rationale:** 244 HP produces 152 HP (divisible by 4) for clean Sitrus Berry activation — Sitrus triggers at exactly 114 HP or below, healing 38 HP. 68 Atk gives meaningful Flare Blitz / Fake Out damage without overinvesting. 100 Def / 96 SpD provides mixed bulk. Total: 508.

### Gardevoir @ Gardevoirite | Trace → Pixilate | Timid | 0 Atk

**Role:** Fast-mode Plan B / Rain coverage / anti-Sneasler nuke.

**Why this pick:** Mega Gardevoir at base 165 SpA (post-Mega) with Timid hits 167 Speed — outspeeds every top-20 threat except Aerodactyl (130) and post-Unburden Sneasler (240). When TR is denied, leads with Incineroar for fast-mode offense. Psychic 4x-KOs Sneasler. Dazzling Gleam spread threatens Garchomp, Dragonite, Tyranitar. Energy Ball covers Rain: hits Pelipper, Basculegion, and Rotom-Wash super-effectively — converts Rain from an autoloss into a manageable matchup. Protect is mandatory for turn-1 survival (without it, Sneasler Fake Out + Kingambit Sucker Punch deletes Gardevoir before it acts).

**Key interactions:** Do NOT bring in TR mode — Gardevoir at 167 Speed moves last under TR. Fast-mode lead: Gardevoir + Incineroar. Fake Out their Pelipper, Energy Ball. Or: Fake Out their Sneasler, Psychic their Garchomp. Protect when position is unclear.

**EV rationale:** 252 SpA maximizes output. 252 Spe maximizes fast-mode speed tier. 4 HP is filler. 0 Atk IV minimizes confusion self-damage and Foul Play interaction.

### Conkeldurr @ Black Belt | Iron Fist | Brave | 0 Spe

**Role:** Secondary physical TR abuser / Fighting priority cleanup.

**Why this pick:** Flame Orb is not legal in Pokemon Champions, so the Guts + Flame Orb combo is off the table. **Iron Fist** boosts punching moves by 20% (Drain Punch, Mach Punch, Ice Punch) — three of Conkeldurr's four moves. **Black Belt** adds a further 20% boost to Fighting-type moves (Close Combat, Drain Punch, Mach Punch). Drain Punch gets both boosts: Iron Fist (1.2) × Black Belt (1.2) = 1.44x effective damage, approximating (but not matching) the old Guts 1.5x boost without requiring a status proc. Close Combat gets only the Black Belt boost (1.2x). Under TR at base 45 Speed, Conkeldurr moves first against the entire meta. Close Combat + Drain Punch threatens 4x-KOs on Sneasler, Kingambit, Tyranitar, and Incineroar.

**Key interactions:** Drain Punch is now the preferred main STAB over Close Combat in most matchups — it takes both item and ability boosts AND heals Conkeldurr. Save Close Combat for when you need the raw damage on a resisted target. Mach Punch (+1 priority) is the out-of-TR finisher for Kingambit — with Iron Fist + Black Belt both active, it hits hard enough to matter. Ice Punch covers Garchomp (4x Ice) and Dragonite (4x Ice) and also benefits from Iron Fist.

**Item note:** Black Belt is the best legal type-boost item for Conkeldurr in Champions. Iron Fist is a legal ability (no change required). This is a net reduction vs Guts/Flame Orb — roughly 1.44x vs 1.5x on Drain Punch, and 1.2x vs 1.5x on Close Combat — but in exchange Conkeldurr does not take 1/16 burn damage per turn, which meaningfully extends longevity across a 5-turn TR window.

**EV rationale:** 252 HP / 252 Atk maximizes bulk and damage simultaneously. 4 Def filler. EV total 508.

## Win Conditions

1. **Primary — Hard TR sweep.** Lead Farigiraf + Incineroar (Armor Tail + Fake Out setup). Incineroar Fake Out flinches their Sneasler or Fake Out user. Farigiraf sets TR under protection. Rhyperior + Conkeldurr clean. Farigiraf Helping Hand amplifies OHKO thresholds. Farigiraf Imprison pre-emptively locks the mirror if they show TR setters.

2. **Secondary — Fast mode (Gardevoir + Incineroar).** Against Rain HO, heavy Taunt, or when TR can't get set. Gardevoir 167 Speed outspeeds the top 20. Energy Ball Pelipper removes rain. Psychic kills Sneasler. Incineroar Fake Out controls turn 1. Works without needing TR activation.

3. **Anti-Meta.** Imprison shuts down the most common TR setter in the format (Sinistcha 68% TR rate). Magic Bounce walls Whimsicott's entire Prankster toolkit. High Horsepower bypasses Pelipper Wide Guard in the Rain matchup. Triple Sneasler coverage (Hatterene Psychic, Gardevoir Psychic, Conkeldurr Close Combat) answers the #2 threat from three independent slots.

## Threats and Weaknesses

**Hard counters:**
- **Mega Floette-Eternal at +2 Calm Mind with Fairy Aura.** No Steel-type on the team. No Fairy resist. At +2, Dazzling Gleam from Fairy Aura threatens the entire team. Prevent the setup: Parting Shot on the CM turn, KO Floette before +2 with Gardevoir Dazzling Gleam or Conkeldurr before it boosts. *Tech fix: Poison Jab over Ice Punch on Conkeldurr if Floette-E dominates locally.*
- **Dual-Imprison stacks (Oranguru + Farigiraf mirror).** If both setters are Imprisoned simultaneously, only fast mode remains. Manageable with correct team preview reads.

**Soft checks:**
- **Kingambit Sucker Punch.** Farigiraf Armor Tail blocks priority on the setup side. Hatterene uses Protect. Conkeldurr Mach Punch (when TR is not active) is the Kingambit punish. Under TR, Conkeldurr moves first and OHKOs with Close Combat — the priority question resolves itself.
- **Charizard-Y Heat Wave before TR is established.** Incineroar Fake Out buys one turn. Get TR up on turn 2. Rock Slide 4x-KOs Charizard under TR.
- **Mega Froslass Aurora Veil.** Incineroar Flare Blitz hits through Veil. Hatterene Psychic bypasses Veil via Magic Bounce protection on Hatterene itself.
- **Scarf Garchomp outside TR (153 Spe).** Gardevoir Dazzling Gleam (4x Dragon), or wait for TR. Ice Punch Conkeldurr is the clean answer under TR.
- **Mega Gengar Perish Trap.** Gardevoir Dazzling Gleam threatens Gengar. Hatterene Protect to stall Perish count. Bring-4 awareness: do not lead both setters when Gengar is in preview.

**Tech options:**
- **Poison Jab over Ice Punch on Conkeldurr.** Floette-E answer — Poison OHKO chance at +0 with Guts. Costs 4x-Ice coverage on Garchomp/Dragonite.
- **Colbur Berry over Mental Herb on Farigiraf.** Survives Kingambit Kowtow Cleave after Intimidate drop, allowing TR on the following turn. Costs Taunt immunity.
- **Protect over Hyper Voice on Farigiraf.** Defensive utility — Farigiraf survives longer. Costs Hyper Voice spread chip (already minimal with 36 SpA EVs anyway).

## Critique & Revisions

### Meta-Coverage Checker Summary

The team's core skeleton (dual-setter TR + Mega Gardevoir Plan B) was correctly targeted for the Reg M-A meta. The Hard TR mirror, Sand Offense, and Semi-Room Balance matchups were evaluated as favorable or even. The two structural failures in the original draft were: (1) zero Rain HO coverage — no Grass or Electric move, with Pelipper Wide Guard blocking both Rhyperior spread moves simultaneously; and (2) Mega Gardevoir lacked Protect, making the Plan B unusable in practice (deleted by Fake Out + Sucker Punch before acting). Additional important-level flags: no Steel-type for Floette-E endgames, Charizard-Y heat damage threatening both Psychic/Fairy setters, and Prankster Encore from Whimsicott creating a disruption window.

### TR Viability Checker Summary

All 8 composition checks passed on the original draft — the skeleton is sound. Weak points flagged (not critical failures): Rhyperior's 4x Water weakness creates a dangerous Rain lead exposure; Hatterene and Gardevoir share Ghost/Steel vulnerability making a paired double-up on that type painful; Rhyperior EQ hits all adjacent allies (no Ground immunity on the team). The checker specifically affirmed the Farigiraf Imprison + Armor Tail core as "close to best-in-class" for Reg M-A.

### Post-Revision Fix: Illegal Items (Champions Item List)

A user-caught defect that neither `tr-architect` nor `speed-math-auditor` detected: the team's original draft assigned **three items that are not legal in Pokemon Champions** — Life Orb (Hatterene), Assault Vest (Rhyperior), and Flame Orb (Conkeldurr). Champions has a restricted 117-item list (see `data/stats/items/champions_items.json`, sourced from serebii.net) that excludes many standard VGC items. Fixes applied:

- **Hatterene: Life Orb → Twisted Spoon.** Psychic damage boost (+20%) preserves the offensive role on her key Sneasler-killing move, with no recoil.
- **Rhyperior: Assault Vest → Passho Berry.** Legal direct answer to the 4x Water weakness flagged by the viability-checker. Solid Rock × Passho on first Water hit = 0.375x damage, converting an OHKO into a healthy tank in the Rain matchup.
- **Conkeldurr: Flame Orb → Black Belt, ability Guts → Iron Fist.** Flame Orb is not legal, so the Guts self-status plan is off the table. Iron Fist + Black Belt stacks on Drain Punch (1.44x), with Black Belt boosting Close Combat (1.2x). Slightly lower peak damage than Guts 1.5x, but no burn chip — better across long TR cycles.

**Upstream fix needed (side-quest):** `tr-architect`, `tr-viability-checker`, and `speed-math-auditor` all need to load and cross-check `data/stats/items/champions_items.json` when building or auditing a team. CLAUDE.md references this file but none of the agent definitions do. Without the cross-check, agents will continue to suggest standard VGC items (Life Orb, Assault Vest, Choice Band, Flame Orb, Rocky Helmet, etc.) that are illegal in Champions.
