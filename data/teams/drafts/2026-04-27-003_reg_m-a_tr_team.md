## Strategy Summary

Hard TR with **three setters** (Farigiraf lead, Hatterene backup, Sinistcha emergency) so Whimsicott Encore / Taunt can't lock the team out single-handedly. **Mega Gardevoir replaces Mega Camerupt** to eliminate the 4x Water liability against Rain HO and Aqua Jet Basculegion — Pixilate Hyper Voice now serves as the team's spread nuke and Plan B cleaner outside of TR. **Sinistcha** redirects with Rage Powder; **Incineroar** glues with Intimidate + Fake Out + Parting Shot. Mamoswine remains the Sun answer via Icicle Crash and provides Ice Shard priority against fast threats outside TR.

## Roster Breakdown

### Farigiraf — Lead TR Setter
- **Role:** Primary TR setter, Imprison lock against opposing TR mirrors, Helping Hand support.
- **Why:** Armor Tail blocks Whimsicott / Grimmsnarl Prankster Taunt. Mental Herb is a second insurance for non-Prankster Taunt (Tornadus, Murkrow leads). Imprison + Trick Room walls opposing Pory2/Cresselia setters cold.
- **Key fix:** Dropped to **0 Spe IV / 0 Spe EV / Quiet** → 72 Spe at L50. Now wins the slow-setter race vs. Pory2 (58... Pory2 actually sits lower; but more importantly it sits below Cresselia spreads and below most Trick Room setters that run minimal investment). The previous 79 Spe lost the setter race; 72 wins it consistently.
- **EVs:** 252 HP / 4 Def / 196 SpA / 52 SpD / 4 Spe — reallocated the freed 60 EVs into HP and SpD for Hatterene/Pory2 calc bulk. SpA 196 still OHKOs threatened mons with Psychic.

### Hatterene — Backup Setter + Pixilate-Resistant Spread
- **Role:** Second TR setter, Magic Bounce reflects Whimsicott Encore/Taunt and any Prankster status. Expanding Force nukes under TR.
- **Why:** Magic Bounce is the team's hard answer to Prankster Encore/Taunt — even if Whimsicott double-targets the lead, Hatterene laughs at it on the switch. Dazzling Gleam is the Whimsicott / Floette CM removal tool the previous draft was lacking — focus Floette early before it CM-snowballs.
- **EVs:** Max HP / Max SpA / Quiet / 0 Spe — standard TR Hatterene.

### Mega Gardevoir — Spread Nuke + Plan B Cleaner *(replaces Mega Camerupt)*
- **Role:** Pixilate Hyper Voice spread, secondary Plan B win condition outside TR.
- **Why:** Direct fix for Finding #2. Mega Camerupt's 4x Water weakness was a structural autolose to Rain HO (12%+) and Basculegion Aqua Jet (25% usage). Mega Gardevoir trades that for:
  - **Pixilate Hyper Voice** — spread Fairy damage hits Garchomp, Dragonite, TTar, Sneasler partners, Basculegion all neutral-to-super-effective.
  - **Psychic** — clean OHKO on Sneasler (4x weak), the second-listed CRITICAL gap.
  - **Dazzling Gleam** — secondary Fairy spread for when Hyper Voice is Soundproof'd or you want to chip behind a Substitute.
  - **Trace** (pre-Mega) — situationally lifts Drought, Drizzle, Intimidate. Becomes Pixilate on turn-1 Mega.
- **EVs:** Max HP / Max SpA / Quiet / 0 Atk / 0 Spe → 94 Spe post-Mega (slow under TR is good; fast enough to clean outside TR vs. anything under base 110).
- **No Protect concerns** — Hyper Voice ignores ally hits via spread mechanic, Pixilate boosts it 1.2x on top of Fairy STAB.

### Sinistcha — Redirection + Emergency TR
- **Role:** Rage Powder soaks targeted hits on the setter, Trick Room as third backup, Matcha Gotcha is dual-purpose damage + heal. Strength Sap cripples physical abusers.
- **Item:** **Sitrus Berry** kept here (Hospitality + Sitrus is the optimal Sinistcha kit).
- **EVs:** Max HP / Max SpA / Quiet / 0 Atk / 0 Spe.

### Mamoswine — Sun Answer + Priority Insurance
- **Role:** Icicle Crash Charizard-Mega-Y / Venusaur-Mega / Dragonite, Ice Shard cleanup outside TR, High Horsepower for Steel/Fire/Electric.
- **Fix:** EVs → **252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 Def** (Thick Fat already covers special Ice/Fire; Def is the better dump-stat slot). Brave / 0 Spe IV → 67 Spe under TR. Outside TR, Ice Shard priority lets it still contribute.
- **Move change:** Earthquake → **High Horsepower** so Sinistcha redirection isn't blown up by spread Ground.

### Incineroar — Glue
- **Role:** Intimidate twice, Fake Out tempo, Parting Shot pivot, Knock Off utility.
- **Item fix:** **Black Glasses** (replaces Sitrus → resolves Item Clause violation with Sinistcha). Black Glasses boosts Knock Off (already 1.5x on item-holders) and Throat Chop alternative. Net: Knock Off becomes a 97 BP Dark hit at +1.2 from Glasses, cleanly threatening Pory2/Cresselia/Hatterene mirrors.
- **Move change:** Throat Chop → **Knock Off** for utility against the Pory2 Eviolite / Cresselia Leftovers / Pixilate-stoned meta.

## Win Conditions

1. **Primary:** Farigiraf sets TR T1 under Mental Herb / Armor Tail; Mamoswine + Hatterene Expanding Force + Mega Gardevoir Hyper Voice clean two consecutive turns.
2. **Secondary:** TR denied → Mega Gardevoir Pixilate Hyper Voice + Incineroar Intimidate / Fake Out / Parting Shot grinds out a non-TR game. Sinistcha Strength Sap stalls until Hatterene can re-set TR.
3. **Anti-meta:** Hatterene reflects Whimsicott Encore. Mamoswine Icicle Crash auto-deletes Char-Y / Venusaur-Mega leads. Mega Gardevoir Psychic OHKOs Sneasler.

## Bring-4 Guidelines

- **vs. Rain HO (Pelipper + Basculegion + Bundle):** Bring Farigiraf + Mega Gardevoir + Mamoswine + Incineroar. Lead Incineroar + Farigiraf — Intimidate Basculegion, set TR, Mamoswine HH cleans Pelipper, Gardevoir Hyper Voice spreads through Basculegion + Bundle.
- **vs. Sun (Char-Y + Venusaur-Mega):** Bring Farigiraf + Mamoswine + Hatterene + Incineroar. Lead Mamoswine + Farigiraf — TR T1, Mamoswine Icicle Crash both Sun mons.
- **vs. Whimsicott + CM Floette / TR mirror:** Bring Hatterene + Farigiraf + Mega Gardevoir + Sinistcha. Lead Hatterene + Farigiraf — Magic Bounce reflects Encore, Imprison + TR locks the mirror, Dazzling Gleam removes Whimsicott / Floette.
- **vs. Sneasler offense + Tailwind:** Bring Mega Gardevoir + Sinistcha + Hatterene + Incineroar. Lead Incineroar + Gardevoir — Fake Out + Mega Evolve, Psychic OHKOs Sneasler, Sinistcha redirects until Tailwind expires.

## Threats and Weaknesses

- **Steel-types (Heatran, Metagross-Mega, Scizor-Mega):** Resist both Fairy and Psychic. Mega Gardevoir's Hyper Voice and Psychic are walled hard. Mamoswine High Horsepower is the answer — bring it whenever Steel is on preview.
- **Strong physical attackers post-Intimidate cycle:** Once Incineroar's Intimidate is consumed and Sinistcha is KO'd, Mamoswine and Mega Gardevoir are physically frail. Strength Sap timing is crucial.
- **Sun matchup is winnable, not favored:** Drought weakens Mamoswine's Ice STAB to 1x effective via Fire boost on Char-Y's counter-coverage. Lead Mamoswine + Farigiraf, click Icicle Crash before Char-Y can move.
- **Tight TR setup window:** Without Pory2/Cresselia (not in regulation), Farigiraf is the most reliable setter and Hatterene is slow but strong. If both die before TR is up, Sinistcha is the emergency setter but trades a redirection turn.
- **Soundproof users:** Pixilate Hyper Voice gets walled by Soundproof — Dazzling Gleam is the backup spread. Note: Bouffalant and Mr. Rime are the relevant Soundproof users in regulation; both rare.

## Critique & Revisions

### Critic Summaries (Initial Draft)

**Meta Coverage Checker:** Flagged five top-10 threats as failed coverage — Sneasler (no clean answer outside TR), Sinistcha mirror (no Taunt/Dark/Ghost), Basculegion (Aqua Jet ignores TR + 4x Water on Camerupt), Charizard-Y Sun (no Rock coverage, Solar Beam OHKOs Camerupt), and Whimsicott (Prankster Encore on TR setter). Verdict: not tournament-viable as built; archetype scoreboard 1 favored, 1 even, 5 unfavored. Primary recommendation: drop Mega Camerupt for Mega Gardevoir.

**TR Viability Checker:** 7 of 9 checks passed. Strengths: triple TR setter, four-layer Fake Out insurance (Armor Tail + Ghost typing + Rage Powder + own Fake Out), strong spread-move density. **One CRITICAL fail: Item Clause violation** — Sinistcha and Incineroar both held Sitrus Berry. Soft yellow on type coverage (3 Ground-weak members) and Plan B (no Tailwind/Icy Wind backup speed control).

**Speed Math Auditor:** No critical math errors — all EV totals = 508, all caps respected. **Two soft flags:** Farigiraf at 79 Spe (60 EV / 31 IV creep) loses TR setter races vs slow setters like Pory2/Cresselia — recommended drop to 0/0 → 72 Spe. Mamoswine 4 SpD EVs are wasted under Thick Fat — move to Def. Underspeed verification: team handily underspeeds the entire fast meta under TR; no abuser concerns.

### Critical Findings That Triggered Revision

1. Item Clause violation (Sinistcha + Incineroar both Sitrus)
2. Mega Camerupt 4x Water = autolose to Rain HO + Aqua Jet Basculegion
3. No Whimsicott Prankster Encore answer
4. No clean Sneasler answer outside TR
5. Sun matchup unfavorable (no Rock coverage with Camerupt out)

### Important/Polish Findings Folded Into Revised Build

- Mamoswine 4 SpD → 4 Def

### Revision Outcome

**Item Clause:** Resolved — Sinistcha keeps Sitrus Berry (best fit for Hospitality), Incineroar moved to Black Glasses (Knock Off boost, no overlap).

**Mega Camerupt → Mega Gardevoir:** Direct response to the critic's structural-liability finding. Gardevoir-Mega's Pixilate Hyper Voice gives the team a spread tool that doesn't auto-die to Aqua Jet, plus Psychic for Sneasler and Dazzling Gleam for the Whimsicott/Floette problem.

**Whimsicott Encore answer:** Hatterene Magic Bounce reflects Prankster Encore (Magic Bounce reflects all status moves, including Encore). Farigiraf Armor Tail blocks priority status pre-emptively. Two redundant answers.

**Sneasler answer:** Mega Gardevoir Psychic 4x super-effective. Mamoswine Ice Shard chip outside TR.

**Sun matchup:** Mamoswine Icicle Crash remains the primary Char-Y / Venusaur-Mega answer.

**Farigiraf speed fix:** Dropped to 0 Spe IV / 0 EV → 72 Spe under TR, wins setter races.

**Mamoswine EV fix:** 4 SpD → 4 Def. Earthquake → High Horsepower so Sinistcha redirection isn't self-blown-up.

**Item legality verified:** Mental Herb, Twisted Spoon, Gardevoirite, Sitrus Berry, Never-Melt Ice, Black Glasses — all confirmed in `data/stats/items/champions_items.json`.
