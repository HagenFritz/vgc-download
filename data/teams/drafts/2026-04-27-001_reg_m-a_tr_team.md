# TR Team Draft — Pokemon Champions Regulation M-A — 2026-04-27-001

## Strategy Summary

Hard-leaning Semi-Room built around Mega Golurk as the primary TR abuser and Competitive Milotic as the dedicated Incineroar punish. Two redundant TR setters (Hatterene with Magic Bounce + Farigiraf with Armor Tail) give us iron-clad answers to Taunt and Sucker Punch — the two biggest TR-denial tools in the format. Plan B is real: Weavile and Tinkaton are both fully functional outside TR, with Weavile providing Fake Out + Ice priority for the Garchomp/Dragonite spam and Tinkaton OHKOing the entire Dragon/Rock skeleton with Gigaton Hammer. We set TR turn 1 against fast offense (Tailwind, Sun, Rain) and play neutral speed against Hard TR mirrors and slow balance.

## Win Conditions

1. **Primary:** Set TR turn 1 with Hatterene (Magic Bounce reflects Taunt) → bring Mega Golurk in turn 2 → Headlong Rush + Poltergeist deletes the Incineroar/Sneasler/Garchomp skeleton. Unseen Fist (post-Mega) means Headlong Rush/Poltergeist/Ice Punch ignore Protect.
2. **Secondary:** No-TR Plan B with Weavile + Tinkaton. Weavile Fake Out + Triple Axel deletes Garchomp/Dragonite, Tinkaton Gigaton Hammer cleans up the rest. Milotic Icy Wind handles speed control. We can win whole games without ever pressing TR.
3. **Anti-Meta:** Competitive Milotic punishes the meta's #1 ability (Incineroar Intimidate, 51% usage) for free +2 SpA boosts. Magic Bounce Hatterene reflects Whimsicott Encore/Taunt back. Mega Golurk Ghost typing eats Fake Out for free turn 1. Dual TR setters mean Imprison cheese (Farigiraf can also Imprison) is mutual MAD.

## Roster Breakdown

### Hatterene
- **Role:** Primary TR setter / spread Fairy attacker
- **Why this pick:** Magic Bounce is the single best anti-Taunt ability in the format, and Whimsicott Prankster Taunt is the most common TR-denial tool in Tailwind teams. Hatterene reflects it back, encoring or taunting their own setter. Base 29 Speed is the second-slowest in the legal pool — no underspeed worries. Mental Herb doubles up on Taunt insurance for the rare Inner Focus Pranksters.
- **Key interactions:** With Mega Golurk in back, Helping Hand isn't needed (Iron Fist already pumps damage); pure spread offense with Dazzling Gleam hits Sneasler 4x and Garchomp/Kingambit (resists matter). Psyshock punches Milotic mirrors and Toxapex through SpD walls. Magic Bounce also reflects Sleep Powder from Venusaur, Will-O-Wisp from Rotom-W, and Encore from Whimsicott — three of the format's most annoying utility moves.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 Def maxes both bulk and the offensive presence she lacks under Magic Bounce's defensive-only reputation. 0 Atk IV minimizes Foul Play / confusion damage. 0 Spe IV + Quiet nature = base-29 floor (effective 31), guaranteed to underspeed every legal Pokemon under TR including opposing Hatterene mirrors at neutral nature.

### Golurk-Mega
- **Role:** Primary TR abuser (USER PINNED)
- **Why this pick:** User constraint, but a legitimately great pick for this meta. Iron Fist pre-Mega boosts Ice Punch by 20% (KOs Garchomp / Dragonite). On Mega Evolution, ability becomes **Unseen Fist** — contact moves bypass Protect, which is enormous in Doubles where Protect is on every team. That means Headlong Rush, Poltergeist, and Ice Punch all push damage through Garchomp Protect, Sneasler Protect, etc. Ground/Ghost typing is also gold: immune to Fake Out (Normal-type), immune to Sucker Punch (Ghost), immune to Extreme Speed from Mega Dragonite (Ghost immune to Normal? Yes — and Ghost in general resists priority through Sucker.). Headlong Rush spreads Ground damage to the entire opposing field except Flying/Levitate.
- **Key interactions:** Don't lead Golurk — bring it in turn 2 after Hatterene sets TR. Headlong Rush hits both opponents but lowers Def/SpD by 1 — partner with Milotic (special) so the SpD drop doesn't nuke our own backline. Poltergeist requires the opponent hold an item, but in this meta with 100% Mega rate + universal Sitrus / White Herb / Focus Sash, that's basically always live. Ice Punch covers Hatterene's Dragon weakness retroactively (Garchomp wall).
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD. 0 Spe IV + Brave (effective 54 Speed) underspeeds every legal TR abuser at neutral nature. Max Atk pushes Headlong Rush + Iron Fist Ice Punch into clean OHKO range on Garchomp / Dragonite / Tyranitar. The SpD breakpoint isn't hit — going to 4 Def vs 4 SpD is a coin flip; we picked SpD because Hyper Voice / Dazzling Gleam from opposing Fairies is the more common shut-down vector.

### Milotic
- **Role:** Competitive Incineroar punish + Icy Wind speed control + bulky water pivot (USER PINNED)
- **Why this pick:** User constraint, and the cleanest anti-meta pick in the format. Incineroar is on 51% of teams and almost universally runs Intimidate (98% of sets). Every time Incineroar switches in next to Milotic, Milotic gains +2 SpA — that turns Scald into a one-shot button against half the meta. Icy Wind is the meta's underused-yet-correct speed control move (Garchomp / Dragonite / Salamence variants all 4x Ice), giving us a secondary Plan B speed mode without committing to Tailwind. Recover keeps her alive across multiple Intimidate cycles.
- **Key interactions:** Do NOT bring Milotic against teams without Incineroar — she's a niche piece, and her Competitive activation wants its target. Double-target a switching Incineroar with Mega Golurk Headlong Rush + Milotic Scald to punish the Parting Shot pivot turn. Icy Wind globally cripples non-Sash fast offense and lets Mega Golurk outspeed if TR ends.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 196 Def / 60 SpD Calm. Bold is more standard, but Calm gives her a SpD edge since Sneasler Dire Claw, Floette Dazzling Gleam, and Sinistcha Matcha Gotcha are all the most common KO threats. 196 Def is enough to survive Sneasler Close Combat from full HP without White Herb shenanigans (Sneasler hits 4x Psychic / 4x Fairy from her partners — but Milotic isn't immune, just not weak). 0 Atk IV minimizes Foul Play. No Speed investment — outside TR she's still base 81 raw, fast enough.

### Farigiraf
- **Role:** Secondary TR setter / anti-priority shield / Helping Hand support
- **Why this pick:** Two reasons. First, Armor Tail blocks ALL priority moves targeting Farigiraf or its partner — including Kingambit Sucker Punch (the format's #1 anti-TR-abuser priority button at 99% usage), Aqua Jet from Basculegion, and Extreme Speed from Mega Dragonite. Second, redundant TR setting: if Hatterene is doubled into and KO'd, Farigiraf comes back later to re-set. Imprison is also available as a tech swap for the Sinistcha TR mirror — running Trick Room + Imprison locks the opponent out of TR for as long as Farigiraf is alive. Hyper Voice ignores Substitute and chips both opponents through Mega Gengar Disable lines.
- **Key interactions:** Pair Farigiraf in back with Mega Golurk — Helping Hand + Headlong Rush is a guaranteed Garchomp OHKO even off Iron Fist (without Helping Hand it's a roll). Farigiraf also Armor Tail-shields Mega Golurk from Sucker Punch on the swap-in turn, which is a critical interaction since Kingambit appears on 28% of teams and Sucker Punch is 99% of its sets.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 132 Def / 124 SpD Sassy. Mixed-bulk spread because the priority moves it absorbs come from both sides (physical Sucker / Aqua Jet, but also priority special moves through Prankster Taunt). 0 Spe IV + Sassy = base-58 raw → ~52 effective, underspeeds Hatterene-mirror neutral natures. Sitrus Berry triggers reliably with Hyper Voice friendly-fire chip.

### Weavile
- **Role:** Plan B fast attacker / Fake Out support / Ice priority for Garchomp + Dragonite
- **Why this pick:** Base 125 Speed is one of the highest in the legal pool, and Weavile's 120 Atk + STAB Triple Axel + STAB Knock Off + Ice Shard priority gives this team a complete second mode that doesn't need TR at all. Triple Axel one-shots Garchomp, Dragonite, Hippowdon, and Mega Altaria. Knock Off cripples item-dependent picks (Sneasler White Herb, Whimsicott Sash, Floette-Mega items can't be knocked but everything else is fair game — and Knock removes Sash before the Sash trigger). Fake Out provides redundant turn-1 control if we're playing without TR. Ice Shard cleans up Dragonite Multiscale survivors and revenge-kills weakened Mega Aerodactyl.
- **Key interactions:** Lead Weavile + Hatterene against Tailwind teams: Fake Out the Tailwind setter (Whimsicott / Aerodactyl), Hatterene Magic Bounce reflects Encore back, then Weavile cleans next turn. Do NOT lead Weavile vs Hard TR — Sucker Punch from Kingambit through Triple Axel is bad math. Knock Off into Sneasler steals the White Herb before it can reset.
- **EV rationale:** Standard 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe Jolly. Base 125 Spe with Jolly + max EVs = 189 effective, outspeeds all of Sneasler-Unburden-trigger (240 still wins, but pre-trigger Sneasler is 200) — caveat: triggered Unburden Sneasler still wins, kill it before White Herb fires. Focus Sash ensures Weavile lives one hit even from Sucker Punch full chip.

### Tinkaton
- **Role:** Plan B Dragon / Rock killer / Steel resist for Fairy spam
- **Why this pick:** Gigaton Hammer is a 160 BP STAB that can't be used twice in a row but OHKOs Garchomp, Dragonite, Tyranitar, Hippowdon, Mega Aerodactyl, and Mega Charizard Y after one Sash break. Mold Breaker bypasses Multiscale on Dragonite, Levitate on Rotom-W, and Sturdy on Archaludon — a critical edge for TR teams that struggle with chip-immune targets. Steel/Fairy resists Floette-E spread Dazzling Gleam, Whimsicott Moonblast, and Sneasler Dire Claw secondary effects. Knock Off provides item-removal redundancy with Weavile. Tinkaton at base 94 Spe is in the awkward middle tier — it's not fast, not slow, but functional.
- **Key interactions:** Pair Tinkaton + Milotic against Rain teams: Tinkaton clicks Gigaton Hammer on Pelipper / Archaludon while Milotic Scalds the field. Tinkaton's Mold Breaker means it ignores Sinistcha Hospitality protection effects, can hit Levitate Rotom-W with Knock Off-no, Knock Off is Dark, doesn't matter, but Mold Breaker still removes the item. Don't lead Tinkaton — bring it in turn 2 after a TR setup or after Weavile chips down a target.
- **EV rationale:** 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 Spe Adamant. Tinkaton is bulky enough at 85/77/105 to take a Sucker Punch from Kingambit and live, then Gigaton Hammer back. 4 Spe lets it creep base-94 mirror tiers. No Speed-hindering nature because outside TR we want Tinkaton functional at full Speed — this is a Plan B piece, not a TR abuser. Sitrus Berry recovers off Sucker Punch chip.

## Bring-4 Guidelines

- **vs. Tailwind Offense (Whimsicott + Aerodactyl + Sneasler + Garchomp):** Hatterene + Weavile + Mega Golurk + Tinkaton. Lead Hatterene + Weavile. Hatterene Magic Bounce reflects Whimsicott Taunt/Encore. Weavile Fake Out the Aerodactyl Tailwind setter to break Sash priority. Set TR turn 2 once tempo is denied. Tinkaton in back to OHKO Garchomp via Gigaton Hammer if it stays in. **Skip Milotic** unless Incineroar is on their team — wasted Competitive trigger otherwise.

- **vs. Trick Room Mirror (Farigiraf + Sinistcha + Torkoal core):** Hatterene + Farigiraf + Mega Golurk + Weavile. Win the setter race with double TR users. Hatterene leads — if they Imprison through Farigiraf, swap Hatterene to Trick Room first. If they double-up on Hatterene, swap to Mega Golurk and let Farigiraf set TR from the back. Weavile Knock Off chips their Sitrus / Mental Herb to break their TR-extension plan. Mega Golurk Poltergeist hits 110 BP minimum and Unseen Fists through Protect.

- **vs. Weather (Rain Pelipper / Bascu / Archaludon):** Hatterene + Milotic + Mega Golurk + Tinkaton. Lead Milotic + Hatterene. Milotic Icy Wind nukes the Drizzle-+-Tailwind speed mode. Hatterene Trick Room turn 1, then Mega Golurk in turn 2 — Headlong Rush deletes Archaludon (4x Ground when not Stamina-stacked) and chips Pelipper. Tinkaton Gigaton Hammer is the cleanup button on a healthy Pelipper. **Watch for Wide Guard Pelipper** — if revealed, single-target the Gigaton Hammer instead of leaning on spread coverage.

- **vs. Sun (Charizard-Y + Venusaur + Incineroar):** Milotic + Mega Golurk + Hatterene + Weavile. Milotic is a HARD lead here — Competitive trigger on Incineroar Intimidate, +2 Scald nukes Charizard (4x Rock isn't relevant; Water STAB +2 deletes anyway). Hatterene Trick Room turn 1, swap Mega Golurk in turn 2 — Headlong Rush hits Incineroar / Venusaur (Venusaur 4x weak via Mega Y but Earthquake-style Headlong Rush is neutral on Grass; Poltergeist on Charizardite-holding Charizard is the better click). Weavile Triple Axel deletes Charizard outright if it's still alive. Magic Bounce on Hatterene also reflects Sleep Powder from Venusaur.

## Threats and Weaknesses

- **Hard counters:**
  - **Mega Gengar Perish Trap** — Shadow Tag locks one of our Pokemon in. Mega Golurk is Ghost-typed, so it can switch out vs Shadow Tag (Ghost types ignore trapping). But Hatterene / Farigiraf / Milotic / Tinkaton are all stuck. If their Politoed brings rain too, double weather problem. **Counterplay:** Lead Mega Golurk + Weavile, KO Gengar before Perish lands turn 4. Weavile Knock Off + Sucker / Triple Axel chips through 92.5% Cursed Body / Sash. Bring Weavile + Mega Golurk + Hatterene + Tinkaton — Hatterene Dazzling Gleam spread (Fairy SE on Gengar's Ghost-Poison? No — Dark resists Fairy, but Gengar is Ghost/Poison, neutral. Psyshock SE on Gengar via Psychic vs Poison — that's the real button).
  - **Mega Excadrill Sand setup** — Tyranitar Sand Stream + Mega Excadrill Sand Rush (effective Spe 176 with Sand Rush). Excadrill Earthquake hits Mega Golurk neutral, Tinkaton 4x Ground. We have NO Ground immunity (Hatterene is Psychic/Fairy, Farigiraf is Normal/Psychic, Weavile is Dark/Ice, Milotic is Water — all neutral or weak). **Counterplay:** Bring Hatterene + Milotic + Weavile + Mega Golurk. Lead Milotic + Weavile, Icy Wind + Fake Out denies Excadrill the Sand Rush trigger turn. Mega Golurk Poltergeist on Tyranitarite is a clean OHKO.

- **Soft checks (uncomfortable but manageable):**
  - **Choice Scarf Basculegion Last Respects** — under TR Bascu is slow, but turn 1 before TR sets, Scarf Bascu Last Respects can pick off Hatterene or Milotic from full. **Mitigation:** Don't lead Hatterene vs revealed Pelipper / Bascu rain. Lead Weavile + Milotic, Fake Out the Pelipper, then set TR with Farigiraf from back.
  - **Mega Floette spread Fairy** — Calm Mind setup + Dazzling Gleam threatens to KO Hatterene / Farigiraf / Weavile. Tinkaton walls it (Steel resists Fairy 0.5x), but if Tinkaton is denied, the team folds. **Mitigation:** Always bring Tinkaton vs Floette teams. Mold Breaker Gigaton Hammer ignores Flower Veil — important — and hits Floette-Mega for the OHKO.
  - **Whimsicott Encore on Trick Room turn** — Prankster Encore catches Hatterene mid-Trick-Room and locks her into TR for 3 turns (a wasted move slot when TR is already up). **Mitigation:** Magic Bounce reflects Prankster Encore, so this is solved at the ability level — but if Whimsicott is Infiltrator (27% of sets), it bypasses Magic Bounce. Mental Herb is the secondary insurance.

- **Pinned-pick weaknesses (USER PREFERENCE — known tradeoffs):**
  - **Mega Golurk takes its Mega slot from a more flexible piece.** Without the Mega slot, the team could run Mega Hatterene (faster TR setter) or Mega Tyranitar (sand cleaner). Mega Golurk's payoff is Unseen Fist + dual STAB spread, but it's a less polished Mega than the alternatives. The tradeoff is acceptable because Headlong Rush + Poltergeist hits the meta's exact weak axis (Ground + Ghost vs the Big Three). **Drawback to surface:** Mega Golurk is 4x Water/Grass/Ghost/Dark/Ice — so Sinistcha Matcha Gotcha (Grass), Basculegion Wave Crash (Water), and Weavile Knock Off (Dark) all 2HKO it. Ice from Weavile / Milotic mirrors is also real (Ice Punch into Mega Golurk hurts). Pre-Mega Golurk has 80 Def / 80 SpD only — leaving it vulnerable on the setup turn. The team plan must protect Mega Golurk turn 1 and Mega Evolve turn 2 immediately; never lead Golurk.
  - **Competitive Milotic is a niche pick that sits dead vs no-Incineroar matchups.** Against Tailwind Sun / Hard TR / Perish Trap variants without Incineroar, Milotic has no Competitive trigger and reverts to a mid-tier Water utility piece. She's still useful for Icy Wind, but her bring-rate drops to ~50% of matchups. The team is over-budgeted for the Incineroar matchup. **Drawback to surface:** Milotic is 4x Grass weak (Sinistcha Matcha Gotcha) and 4x Electric weak (Archaludon Electro Shot under Rain — instant-charge, OHKOs through 252/196 bulk). She also has no priority and base-81 Speed without Tailwind support — she's outpaced by Garchomp / Dragonite / Aerodactyl. Bring her vs Incineroar teams; bench her vs Rain HO unless Sneasler is on the field as a pseudo-Intimidate-bait.

- **Tech options:**
  - **Swap Farigiraf's Hyper Voice → Imprison** for the Hard TR mirror. Imprison + Trick Room locks the opponent out of TR entirely.
  - **Swap Tinkaton's Knock Off → Encore** if facing setup-heavy teams (Mega Floette Calm Mind, Sneasler Swords Dance variants).
  - **Swap Weavile's Ice Shard → Throat Chop** for Perish Trap matchups (silences Perish Song).
  - **Swap Hatterene's Psyshock → Helping Hand** if running Mega Golurk + Hatterene as a more aggressive lead pair (Helping Hand + Headlong Rush is a guaranteed Mega TTar OHKO).
